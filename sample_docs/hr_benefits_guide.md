# HR Benefits, Compensation & Payroll Administration Guide

## 1. Salary Banding and Compensation Reviews
Annual performance appraisals and salary band calibrations occur in Q4 of each fiscal year. Managers submit compensation adjustment proposals through the internal HR portal. All salary adjustments exceeding 15% require dual approval from the Department Head and Chief People Officer.

## 2. Health Insurance and Retirement Matching
Full-time employees receive comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage starting on day 1 of employment. The company provides a dollar-for-dollar 401(k) retirement contribution match up to 6% of total base salary.

## 3. Discretionary Bonus Allocations
Discretionary performance bonuses are awarded based on individual OKR achievements and company EBITDA targets. Bonus payouts occur biannually in March and September.
