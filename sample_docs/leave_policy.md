# Enterprise Leave and Time-Off Policy

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees are allocated 25 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per month. Unused PTO up to a maximum of 5 days can be carried forward to the following calendar year.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave annually. For consecutive absences exceeding 3 business days, a medical certificate signed by a licensed practitioner is required upon return to work.

## 3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months.
